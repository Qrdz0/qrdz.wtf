Last updated 25 November 2026

No tracking pixels, no analytics scripts and no cookies. No personal data is collected, stored, or sold through this site.

Contact

If you email me at unbanqrdz@gmail.com, your email address and message will only be used to reply to you. It will never be shared with third parties.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. I can't see or access those logs. That is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts are self-hosted on this site, so no font provider receives your IP address.

GDPR

As a Belgian resident, I respect GDPR. Since no data is collected, there is no data to delete, but you can contact me with any questions.

If you have emailed me, you can ask to see or delete that email at any time. You also have the right to complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority at dataprotectionauthority.be.
